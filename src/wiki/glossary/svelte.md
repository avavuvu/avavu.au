<Wikipedia title="Svelte"/>

**Svelte** is my preferred Javascript framework. It makes interactive websites much easier to create. I've used it in most of my web projects, including [[Radiofodder.live]], and my web based games, [[Crossword.blue]], [[Suburble]], and [[Beat Me At Boggle]].

Of the three big Javascript frameworks—[[WP:React (software)|React]], [[WP:Vue.js|Vue]], and Svelte—Svelte is the youngest. Its age means it has the least amount of baggage, and it was able to be designed in the most streamlined way. The downside being it has the worst ecosystem of the three.

I'm a fan of Svelte's creator, Rich Harris. I liked his talk, [What You Can Do For Your Framework](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xCeYmdukOKI).
