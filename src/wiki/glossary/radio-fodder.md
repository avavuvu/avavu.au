**Radio Fodder** is the student radio station at the University of Melbourne, under [[Farrago Magazine]]. I was directly involved between 2023 and 2025, and I'm still involved indirectly as the webmaster of their website, [[radiofodder.live]].

I don't believe that either of my shows, Belly of a Whale, and Things I Think, have any digital footprint. I'm not sure I'd want you to listen either. I think my skills as a presenter have improved greatly since first starting radio. I'd recommend joining your local radio station, especially if you have social anxiety.
