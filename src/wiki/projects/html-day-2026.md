**HTML Day Melbourne** was an event I ran where people got together to write [[HTML]] code. It was part of a [global network of HTML Day events.](https://2026.html.energy/) I co-hosted it with Amelie Vorchheimer at Palomino Bar in Brunswick.

It was a huge success. Thank you to everyone that came. I have plans to host more in the coming years.
