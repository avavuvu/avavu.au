**Exhaust** was my final project for my high school Media Studies class. It is a comic book about climate change, with 3D backgrounds rendered in [[Blender]] and 2D characters I drew in pencil and scanned.

It is about politics and climate change, centred around a poem I wrote. I made it when I was 17, and you can tell, but I am still very proud of it. [Read it here.](/downloads/exhaust.pdf)
