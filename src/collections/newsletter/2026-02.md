# How are you doing, {{name}}?

This year, to preempt my latent fear of ageing, I decided to soft launch being 23 months before I actually turned 23. 23 is scary age, because it's the first year people stop writing songs about. For [22](https://soundcloud.com/gabrieldvarte/taylor-swift-22-dj-biel-baile-edit-v2), [21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sRxrwjOtIag&pp=ygURQWxsIFRvbyBXZWxsIC0gVFM%3D), [20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hbszWBzYkPo), [19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=08DjMT-qR9g), [18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TaLfih7rq6U&pp=ygUUZWlnaHRlZW4gYmVhYmFkb29iZWU%3D), especially [17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xFrGuyw1V8s&pp=ygUNZGFuY2luZyBxdWVlbg%3D%3D), or [16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HyQ8e0jmGoc&pp=ygUQc2l4dGVlbiBkaWV0IGNpZ9IHCQmRCgGHKiGM7w%3D%3D), there are so many songs. 23? Not so much. When I actually turned 23, it was very nice. I spent it away with some friends. We played a lot of Boggle. It turns out I love Boggle.
