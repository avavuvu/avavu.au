My readership is quite small so I've only made $0.04, which hasn't been enough to cover server fees. Still, it's been worth it to understand how Jeff Bezos feels every day.

## OULD

When I was a kid, I could never spell the second half of 'could', 'should', or 'would' with any reliability, so I came up with the mnemonic, _Oh You Lucky Duck._ But today, it is **you** who is the lucky duck. You are getting this month's newsletter one whole day early, because I am leaving for Vietnam tomorrow for a holiday. Don't fret, I'll be back before long. I've been tossing up bringing my laptop, and I've decided not to live the digital nomad life. It's probably important for me to not be working on something for a few weeks. This does mean if something breaks there is no fixing it. There should be enough Suburbles to last until I'm back.
