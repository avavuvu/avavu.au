I don't enjoy driving, despite having a license, so most of the time I get around with **public transport**. I am an advocate for improved public transport. I think public transport is better for your health and better for your city's health.

I love the feeling of coming home on a bustling train, where people are all laughing with their friends in the four-seaters, spilling over into other seats. I love when I've delivered flowers to a friend or family member via public transport, and every other commuter comments on it. And I just think trains and trams are awesome.
