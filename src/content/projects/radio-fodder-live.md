**radiofodder.live** is the official website of [[Radio Fodder]]. 

## Version One
The first version of the website launched in March of 2025. I was told by the managers to make something stupidly simple. I remember being told to make the directory of shows literally just a list. It was inspired heavily by the website [utrecht.jp](https://utrecht.jp). It used a three-colour design of white and black with light blue accents.

The schedule page featured traced horse silhouettes very prominently. I wanted to counterbalance the chicness of the website with a really weird design element. The website was serving a low-budget university community so it needed to look handmade.

The website was written in Vue with a simple Python backend. Radio Fodder uses Radiocult as its CMS.

## Version Two
The website was redesigned in August 2026. I turned the three-colour layout into a strict two-colour layout.

I switched the website to use [[Svelte]] and SvelteKit.
